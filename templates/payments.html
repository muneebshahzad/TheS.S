How these figures work

Expected net COD is the shipment amount less DigiDokaan delivery, other and reserve deductions. It is not money received.

Amount paid is the actual amount DigiDokaan reports as paid. Only delivered, unpaid shipments are counted as pending settlement; active shipments remain awaiting delivery.
