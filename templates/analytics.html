How to read this report

    Delivery rate = delivered ÷ (delivered + cancelled). Cancellation rate uses the same denominator. In-process orders are excluded from both rates.

    Cost per delivered order = spend ÷ delivered orders. Delivered ROAS = delivered revenue ÷ spend. A dash means unavailable or a zero denominator.
